03The problem

A score is not an explanation.

Verity's AI verifies whether a document satisfies a requirement, and it is sometimes wrong. A confidence number asks the reviewer to believe a verdict they cannot check. In compliance, acting on a wrong one fails the audit.

Trust is not a component. It's a system of patterns.

Six of them. Remove one and trust breaks somewhere in the flow
04The overview

Every requirement, one surface.

Status, owner and due date for all forty, so a gap is visible long before the deadline is.

05Evidence

Evidence, not verdicts.

Every criterion opens to the exact text the AI read, the page it came from, and one line on what it found. The label says what the AI found, never why this matches. Whether the evidence is enough stays a human call.

06The reasoning

The reasoning arrives first.

Criteria resolve one at a time as the document is read. By the time the verdict lands it is a summary of what was already on screen, not the output of a spinner.

07Uncertainty

It names what it can't confirm.

Not verification failed. The exact gap: ISO 14064 is cited, but the document never states whether Scope 2 was market-based or location-based. The criterion is flagged for review and links to the section the AI read.

08Override

Override is first-class.

Going against the AI takes a written reason. The note is logged beside the original flag, in sequence, timestamped. The finding is never deleted. Both layers stay visible to whoever checks this decision later.

09The system

Six patterns. Remove one and trust breaks.

01
Extracted source text
The raw evidence, not the conclusion. Verifiable, not assumed.
02
Page and section citation
Exactly where to look. No manual searching.
03
Graduated states
Verified, needs review and wrong document, each with its own weight.
04
Specific uncertainty
Names the gap precisely. An explanation, not a status.
05
First-class override
A legitimate action with its own workflow, not an emergency exit.
06
Preserved AI reasoning
Override adds a layer. It never erases. Both live in the trail.
