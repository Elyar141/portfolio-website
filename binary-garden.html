Case Study

Binary
Garden

Live data streams rendered as breathing organisms. Market noise made into something that moves, and something you can almost feel.

Project Visual

Overview

Binary Garden translates financial market data into generative, organic forms. The project started as a question: what if volatility felt like weather, not warnings? What if you could sense the market's mood before reading a single number?

Problem

Data dashboards overwhelm. Numbers compound into noise. Traders and analysts spend cognitive energy parsing representations rather than interpreting meaning. The visual language of finance: red, green, bars, lines. Efficient but emotionally inert.

Solution

A living visualization layer that maps market state to biological movement patterns. Volatility becomes breath rate. Volume becomes density. Momentum becomes directionality in growth patterns. The system is ambient: legible at a glance, readable in depth.

System Detail

Outcome

Exhibited at two data art events. Several trading desks requested private builds. The feedback that stuck: "I noticed the crash three minutes early because something felt wrong with the garden." That was the whole point.
