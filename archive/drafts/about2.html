I keep thinking I will arrive. And I do.Then I look up and the road just keeps going.Turns out that is the whole job.

The first drive, I barely see the road.Just enough of it to stay on.

Second time through, things start showing up.A sign I drove straight past. A turn that was always there.

Nobody puts that part on the map.You have to drive it a few times before you can see it.

And everything I finally see, I paint in.The small things. The ones nobody notices until they go missing.

The road never gets finished.It just gets more coloured in.

So I keep driving.Same road. I can just see more of it now.
