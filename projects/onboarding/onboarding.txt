Redesigning Studio's onboarding, from zero guidance to self-serve in under 2 minutes.
01. The Challenge
Studio was never built for strangers
Originally an invite-only internal tool. No sign-up, no welcome, no guidance. The app assumed you already knew what you were doing.

What new users hit immediately:

No sign-up flow, no guided setup, just a blank app grid

Had to understand Company → Project hierarchy before doing anything

8+ required fields just to create a company (VAT ID, legal address, representative…

Empty state with zero fallback, if you had nothing, you saw nothing




The original landing screen, tool tiles with no context, no entry point for new users
02. Research
What other platforms do right
Audited Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Ghost, Shopify, WordPress and others. Four patterns appeared everywhere:


Intent question first : "What are you building?" before any editor

Social login: Google/Microsoft SSO removes the email friction entirely

Straight to editor: skip the dashboard, land in the canvas

Pre-seeded content: never show a blank screen on first login



Nine platforms audited, four patterns showed up in every successful onboarding.
03. Design evolution
Three iterations before the right model
users think in terms of what they're building, not how the data is organised. Every flow that led with "Company" was adding friction without adding value.




Still container-first. Create a company before a project. No guidance



One entry point — but forced users to choose between internal data concepts on day one.
04. The solution
Project first
After login, new users are guided through a short conversational wizard, four steps, all skippable. It asks how they want to start, names their company and first project, and routes them straight to the dashboard. No architecture exposed, no heavy forms, no empty state.


Project tiles are the primary navigation, company name is a secondary label

Company filter dropdown replaces tabs, works at any scale

"My First Project" always pre-seeded, no empty state possible

One click on any tile goes straight to the Editor




"Four steps from login to first project: skippable, conversational, no forms.


Always creating a project. Company is a field, not a gate.
Results

New users reach their first project in under 2 minutes

No user ever lands on an empty screen

Company hierarchy fully hidden on day one,  zero architecture exposed

Scales to any number of clients without navigation breaking

Reduced required fields from 8+ to 2 to start working
