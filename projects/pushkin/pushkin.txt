Café Puschkin is a Leipzig café and cultural venue in the Südvorstadt district. Named after the Russian poet Alexander Pushkin, it needed a visual identity that connected its literary heritage with Leipzig's contemporary creative scene.


Visual identity and website design for a Leipzig café and cultural venue.
Working with the team at Lilaquadrat, I designed the visual identity system and website using Soviet constructivist aesthetics as inspiration. Bold condensed typography and a warm color palette create a strong visual language across desktop and mobile. The design honors the café's cultural roots while working as a functional website for daily operations.
