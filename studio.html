Studio is a B2B content platform: companies, their projects, and the apps inside each project. I rebuilt how you move through it. Projects come first, and one panel switches project or app from any screen.

Overview

Problem

Every level looked the same, so nobody knew where they were.

Company, project and app were the same flat tile. The company page and the project page both showed a grid called Apps. Only the breadcrumb told you which level you were on.

Before

Seven clicks to open the same app in another project.

Customers → project → company → all companies → the other company → its project → Customers. The last click reopens the app you were already in.

Decision 1 · Remove a level

Projects first. The company level is gone.

People work inside a project, so the dashboard lists projects. Company became a label on each card and a filter in the header.

Decision 2 · One global panel

Switch project or app from any screen.

One level fewer still leaves a climb. The panel removes it: this project's apps and every other project sit behind one bar at the top of every screen. Pick another project and you stay on the screen you were on. This is why the two decisions only work together.

After

The same task in three clicks, and you never leave Customers.

Open the panel, pick the other project, done. App Store, Team and Media are one more pick each, from the same panel, with no trip back to the dashboard.

Across Studio

The same rules on every screen.

Result

Seven clicks became three. Three levels became one.

Counted from matched recordings of the same task in both versions. Shipped to live customers with no downtime.

What I'd measure next

Time to a first published item, how often support gets asked where something is, and whether people switch from the panel or still go back to the dashboard.
