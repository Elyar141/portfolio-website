02The problem

Nothing told you where you were going.

No photography, no progression, no hierarchy — and all four programmes looked identical. Every fault produced the same feeling: an administrative task.

It should feel like the beginning of a journey. Not like filing a form.

Every decision that follows comes out of that one line
03Before

A faded map and five identical cards.

Logging in took two screens over a washed-out world map. The dashboard gave five teal cards the same weight, with a half-finished application buried somewhere behind them.

04The first screen

It says where you're going.

Email and password collapse onto one screen, over a full-bleed photograph of the Amber Fort. Before anyone types a character, they know what this is for.

05The dashboard

Two cards worth dwelling on.

A personal greeting, then two hero programmes carrying destination photography and an apply button. Everything secondary drops to a text link — photography means dwell, text means utility.

06One question

Who is this application for?

Applicants often apply for a family member, so the form asks first. Two cards, one already carrying an in-progress badge. Continue stays inactive until one is picked.

07Inside the form

Eight tabs became one step.

Step one now opens with a welcome, every destination with its dates and price, and a progress bar that exists before the first field.

08The visual language

Six decisions, carried everywhere.

01
Cabinet Grotesk
Geometric, with warmth. Replaces a generic extra-light sans.
02
Sub-brand colour
Each programme keeps its own accent. Teal is reserved for anything interactive.
03
Photography as context
Used where dwelling is right, withheld where speed is.
04
No rounded corners
Sharp edges. Editorial rather than generic SaaS.
05
Cream, not white
#F8F7F5 for the page; white is kept for containers and cards.
06
One decision per screen
Progressive disclosure instead of everything at once.
Set in milestone one and carried unchanged through the rest of the platform
